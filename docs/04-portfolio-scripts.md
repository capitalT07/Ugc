# Portfolio: six concept ads

Six sample ads to show brands your style before you have paying clients. Three you can film yourself without your face (2, 3, 5); three use a roster creator for the on-camera part, with your faceless b-roll (1, 4, 6).

## Labelling rules (non-negotiable)

- Every sample is **concept work**. Put a small corner label on the video: `Concept ad · إعلان تجريبي`, and write under it in the portfolio: *"Concept ad made with a product we bought. Not sponsored by or affiliated with the brand."*
- Use products you or the creator **actually bought and used**. Every line in a script must be true for the person saying it. Where a script has `[…]`, fill in the real answer, even if it's less impressive.
- Don't post these as ads for a real brand on public accounts without the brand's permission. They live in the portfolio.
- Shooting a short concept with a top lead's product (Cotmasr, Sigma Fit, Mr.Beard) is a strong door-opener, but it's your choice and your cost. Never do it because a client asked for free work.

## What you need

A phone with a clean lens, a cheap tripod with a phone clip, a window, a table, and a quiet room. Optional: a clip-on microphone and a small LED light. Shoot vertical (9:16), 4K or 1080p at 30 fps, back camera, 1x lens (not ultra-wide).

## Editing defaults in CapCut (all six)

1. New project, **9:16**. Import clips in shot order.
2. **Hook text** on screen from 0.0 to 1.5 s: big bold text, 4–7 words, in the middle of the frame.
3. Cut every pause; aim for a new shot or a zoom every 1–2 seconds. Punch in (scale 110–115%) on key words.
4. **Captions:** Text → Auto captions (Arabic or English), then fix every error by hand. Keep text inside the safe zone: away from the bottom 20% and the right edge, where TikTok and Reels put their buttons.
5. **Sound:** voice clear and loud; music at about 15–20% volume under the voice. For client ads, use music cleared for commercial use (TikTok Commercial Music Library or royalty-free tracks marked for commercial use), never trending copyrighted songs.
6. **Colour:** small exposure and warmth fix only; skin should look natural.
7. **End card** (last 2 s): product shot + CTA text.
8. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps, high bitrate. Delete CapCut's ending clip.
9. Make **3 versions with different hooks** (just swap the first 2 seconds). That's your "3 hooks per ad" selling point.

## 1. Product review: "First time trying beard oil, honest take"

- **Who films:** roster creator (man with a beard) for talking shots; you for hands and product close-ups.
- **Shows brands:** honest review format; fits Mr.Beard, Meraki, The.Man.Brand.
- **Length:** 25–30 s.

**Hooks (pick one, cut the other two as alternates)**
1. AR: «دقني كانت ناشفة وبتهرش… فجربت ده لأول مرة قدامكم» · EN: "My beard was dry and itchy, so I tried this for the first time on camera."
2. AR: «٣ غلطات بيعملها أغلب الرجالة مع زيت الدقن» · EN: "3 mistakes most guys make with beard oil."
3. AR: «هل زيت الدقن بيفرق فعلاً؟» · EN: "Does beard oil actually make a difference?"

**Script (Egyptian Arabic)**
> [0–3] دقني كانت ناشفة وبتهرش… فجربت زيت الدقن ده لأول مرة قدامكم.
> [3–9] أول حاجة: الريحة خفيفة ومش بتخنق. نقطتين بس على إيدي… وأدعكها من الجذور كده.
> [9–15] مش بيسيب لمعة زيت، والدقن بقت أنعم من أول مرة.
> [15–22] هل هيخلّي دقني تطلع أسرع؟ معرفش، ومش هقولك حاجة مش متأكد منها. بس الهرش راح والشكل بقى أنضف.
> [22–27] لو دقنك ناشفة زيي، اللينك تحت.

**Script (English)**
> [0–3] My beard was dry and itchy, so I'm trying this beard oil for the first time on camera.
> [3–9] First thing: the smell is light, not overpowering. Two drops in my hand... and work it in from the roots.
> [9–15] No oily shine, and it already feels softer.
> [15–22] Will it make my beard grow faster? I don't know, and I won't claim that. But the itch is gone and it looks neater.
> [22–27] If your beard's dry like mine, link below.

**Shot list**

| # | Time | Framing | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3 s | Medium close-up, chest up | Holds bottle near face, says the hook | Creator |
| 2 | 3–5 s | Extreme close-up | Two drops land in a palm | You (hands) |
| 3 | 5–9 s | Lower-face close-up | Rubs oil in from the roots | Creator |
| 4 | 9–13 s | Close-up | Brushes beard; light catches the texture | Creator |
| 5 | 13–22 s | Medium close-up | Honest verdict to camera | Creator |
| 6 | 22–27 s | Product beauty shot | Bottle on a shelf, slow push-in | You |

**Camera:** tripod at eye level, 60–70 cm away for talking shots; 15–20 cm for close-ups. **Light:** face a window; switch off the yellow ceiling light so colours don't mix. **Edit:** hook text «دقني كانت ناشفة 😩»; soft drop sound on shot 2; punch-in on «مش بيسيب لمعة»; end card "Concept ad".

**Caption:** AR «جربت زيت الدقن لأول مرة… رأيي بصراحة 👇 #عناية_الرجالة #دقن» · EN "First time trying beard oil: honest take 👇" · **CTA:** «اطلبه من اللينك» / "Tap the link to try it."

## 2. Unboxing: "I ordered from a local brand. This is what showed up."

- **Who films:** you, faceless (hands only).
- **Shows brands:** packaging, fabric, first impressions; fits Cotmasr, Kijaqo, Sigma Fit, Gym Apparel, streetwear.
- **Length:** 20–28 s. Film it the day a real order arrives (buy one basic item from a local brand).

**Hooks**
1. AR: «طلبت من براند مصري… وده اللي وصلني» · EN: "I ordered from a local brand. This is what showed up."
2. AR: «صوت فتح الطلبية بس 🔊» · EN: "Just the sound of unboxing 🔊"
3. AR: «يستاهل السعر؟ نفتحه سوا» · EN: "Worth the price? Let's open it."

**Script:** mostly on-screen text with natural sound (ASMR). Optional whispered voice-over:

> **AR (نص على الشاشة):** طلبت من براند مصري… خلينا نشوف ← التغليف ← الخامة (لقطة قريبة) ← التقفيل ← المقاس على الجسم ← رأيي: الخامة [✅/❌] · التقفيل [✅/❌] · المقاس [مظبوط/واسع/ضيق] ← اسم البراند في أول كومنت
>
> **EN (on-screen text):** I ordered from a local brand… let's see → packaging → fabric (close-up) → stitching → fit → my verdict: fabric [✅/❌] · stitching [✅/❌] · size [true / big / small] → brand name in the first comment

Fill the verdict honestly. A "runs small" note makes the video more believable, not less.

**Shot list**

| # | Time | Framing | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2 s | Top-down | Package on the table, hands tap it twice |
| 2 | 2–6 s | Top-down, close | Tape pulled slowly (ASMR) |
| 3 | 6–10 s | Top-down | Lid opens, tissue unfolds, item revealed |
| 4 | 10–16 s | Macro, side light | Fabric stretched, stitching, inside tag |
| 5 | 16–22 s | Mirror, neck-down | Item held against body, then worn |
| 6 | 22–28 s | Top-down | Item folded next to the box; verdict text |

**Camera:** phone on a tripod arm above the table (or held straight down), plus one 45° side angle. **Light:** table beside a window; white or wooden table; no overhead light. **Edit:** no music for the first 6 seconds (let the sound sell it), soft music after the reveal; speed-ramp the unfolding; verdict ticks appear one by one.

**Caption:** AR «فتحت طلبيتي من براند مصري 📦 رأيكم؟» · EN "Unboxing a local order 📦 Thoughts?" · **CTA:** «اسم البراند في أول كومنت» / "Brand name in the first comment."

## 3. Product demo: "Plan a week of workouts in 2 minutes" (app, no shipping)

- **Who films:** you, faceless (screen recording, hands, neck-down gym shots), with your voice-over.
- **Shows brands:** app and software demos; fits FitXpert, ElCoach, the Upwork app jobs. No shipping, so it works for Gulf and international clients.
- **App:** a free fitness or habit app you actually use. Say its real name; label the video as a concept.
- **Length:** 30–40 s.

**Hooks**
1. AR: «لو بتروح الجيم ومش عارف هتعمل إيه النهارده… شوف دي» · EN: "If you walk into the gym with no plan, watch this."
2. AR: «جدول تمرين أسبوع كامل في دقيقتين» · EN: "A full week of workouts in 2 minutes."
3. AR: «بطلت أكتب تمريني في النوتس» · EN: "I stopped writing my workouts in Notes."

**Script (Egyptian Arabic, voice-over)**
> [0–3] لو بتروح الجيم ومش عارف هتعمل إيه النهارده… شوف دي.
> [3–10] فتحت [التطبيق]، اخترت الهدف: [الهدف]، [عدد] أيام في الأسبوع.
> [10–18] في أقل من دقيقة طلعلي جدول كامل… وكل تمرين معاه فيديو يوضّح الحركة.
> [18–26] بعد كل سِت بسجّل الوزن… والتطبيق بيقولي أزوّد إمتى.
> [26–33] أنا بستخدمه بقالي [مدة حقيقية]، ودي أكتر حاجة نظمت تمريني.
> [33–38] جرّبه — اللينك تحت.

**Script (English)**
> [0–3] If you walk into the gym with no plan, watch this.
> [3–10] I opened [app], picked my goal, [goal], [number] days a week.
> [10–18] In under a minute it built a full plan, and every exercise has a video showing the movement.
> [18–26] After each set I log the weight, and it tells me when to go heavier.
> [26–33] I've used it for [real period], and it's what finally organised my training.
> [33–38] Try it. Link below.

**Script (French)**
> [0–3] Tu arrives à la salle sans programme ? Regarde ça.
> [3–10] J'ouvre [app], je choisis mon objectif, [objectif], [nombre] jours par semaine.
> [10–18] En moins d'une minute, j'ai un programme complet, avec une vidéo pour chaque exercice.
> [18–26] Après chaque série, je note le poids, et l'appli me dit quand augmenter.
> [26–33] Je l'utilise depuis [durée réelle], et c'est ce qui a enfin organisé mon entraînement.
> [33–38] Essaie-la. Lien en dessous.

**Shot list**

| # | Time | Framing | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3 s | Neck-down, gym | Standing at a rack, looking at the phone |
| 2 | 3–10 s | Screen recording | Choosing goal and days (record with the phone's built-in screen recorder) |
| 3 | 10–18 s | Screen recording | Plan appears; tap into one exercise video |
| 4 | 18–22 s | Over-the-shoulder | Logging a set on the phone between sets |
| 5 | 22–26 s | Neck-down | Doing the set |
| 6 | 26–38 s | Phone on bench, hand picks it up | Verdict and CTA |

**Camera:** gym shots at chest height with the phone on a tripod or leaning on a bench; ask the gym before filming and keep other people out of frame. **Light:** daylight near gym windows; avoid filming straight into ceiling lights. **Edit:** zoom in on each tap; add a tap circle; one split-screen (app + you lifting); record the voice-over in a quiet room afterwards and lay it over.

**Caption:** AR «جدول تمرين كامل في دقيقتين 💪 #جيم #تمرين» · EN "A week of training planned in 2 minutes 💪" · FR « Un programme complet en 2 minutes 💪 » · **CTA:** «حمّله من اللينك» / "Download from the link."

## 4. Storytelling: "The compliment test" (fragrance)

- **Who films:** roster creator (talking shots and day clips); you film product shots.
- **Shows brands:** story structure (problem → test → result); fits Nspired, Nuit, Breathe, Boshara, Bubblzz.
- **Rule:** the count must be real. If the result is zero, either keep it as an honest, funny ad or re-film with another scent. Never fake it.
- **Length:** 30–40 s, filmed across one real day.

**Hooks**
1. AR: «بحط برفان كل يوم… ومحدش عمره قالي ريحتك حلوة. النهارده هعمل اختبار» · EN: "I wear perfume every day and nobody has ever complimented it. Today I'm testing that."
2. AR: «رشّتين الساعة ٩… هعد كام واحد هيعلق» · EN: "Two sprays at 9 am. I'm counting compliments."
3. AR: «الساعة ٥ العصر: لسه الريحة موجودة؟» · EN: "5 pm: can you still smell it?"

**Script (Egyptian Arabic)**
> [0–4] بحط برفان كل يوم… ومحدش عمره قالي «ريحتك حلوة». فالنهارده هعمل اختبار.
> [4–9] رشّتين من [البرفان] الساعة ٩ الصبح، وهعد كام واحد هيعلّق.
> [9–20] (لقطات اليوم: الشغل، الكافيه، العربية — والعداد على الشاشة يزيد مع كل تعليق حقيقي)
> [20–26] الساعة ٥ العصر… (يشم معصمه) لسه موجودة؟ [الإجابة الحقيقية].
> [26–32] النتيجة: [العدد الحقيقي] تعليقات. لو بتدوّر على ريحة بتتلاحظ، جرّبه.
> [32–36] اسمه واللينك تحت.

**Script (English)**
> [0–4] I wear perfume every day and nobody has ever said "you smell good". So today I'm running a test.
> [4–9] Two sprays of [perfume] at 9 am, and I'm counting how many people mention it.
> [9–20] (day clips: work, café, car; the counter on screen goes up with each real compliment)
> [20–26] 5 pm... (smells wrist) still there? [real answer].
> [26–32] Final count: [real number]. If you want a scent people notice, try it.
> [32–36] Name and link below.

**Script (French)**
> [0–4] Je mets du parfum tous les jours et personne ne m'a jamais dit « tu sens bon ». Alors aujourd'hui, je fais un test.
> [4–9] Deux pulvérisations de [parfum] à 9 h, et je compte les compliments.
> [9–20] (moments de la journée : bureau, café, voiture ; le compteur monte à chaque vrai compliment)
> [20–26] 17 h… (sent son poignet) ça tient encore ? [vraie réponse].
> [26–32] Résultat : [vrai nombre]. Si tu cherches un parfum qu'on remarque, essaie-le.
> [32–36] Le nom et le lien en dessous.

**Shot list**

| # | Time | Framing | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 s | Medium close-up | Hook to camera, bottle in hand | Creator |
| 2 | 4–6 s | Extreme close-up | Spray in slow motion, backlit by the window | You |
| 3 | 6–9 s | Medium | Checks watch: 9:00 | Creator |
| 4 | 9–20 s | Mixed | 3–4 day moments, counter overlay | Creator (selfie or a friend films) |
| 5 | 20–26 s | Close-up | Smells wrist at 5 pm | Creator |
| 6 | 26–36 s | Medium close-up + product shot | Result and CTA | Creator + you |

**Camera and light:** eye-level tripod for talking shots by a window; day clips can be handheld selfie-style (that's the format). Never film strangers without permission; blur any face that appears. **Edit:** counter graphic top-left; timestamp text for each scene; music builds slightly towards the result.

**Caption:** AR «اختبار المجاملات 🧪 النتيجة في الآخر» · EN "The compliment test 🧪 Result at the end" · FR « Le test des compliments 🧪 » · **CTA:** «اسمه واللينك تحت» / "Name and link below."

## 5. Lifestyle: "POV: 6:30 am in Cairo" (coffee or snack)

- **Who films:** you, faceless POV.
- **Shows brands:** a product placed naturally in a routine; fits SLVRSKN, Social Coffee Roasters, Brüz, Beast.
- **Length:** 20–25 s, mostly music, on-screen text, very little voice.

**Hooks**
1. AR: «POV: الساعة ٦:٣٠ الصبح في القاهرة» · EN: "POV: 6:30 am in Cairo."
2. AR: «روتيني الصباحي بـ٣ حاجات بس» · EN: "My morning routine, 3 things only."
3. AR: «قبل الجيم بـ٢٠ دقيقة» · EN: "20 minutes before the gym."

**Script (on-screen text, one line per shot)**
> **AR:** ٦:٣٠ ← المنبه ← قهوة [المنتج] في ٣ دقايق ← شنطة الجيم: [المنتج] + مياه ← الشارع لسه فاضي ← آخر سِت ← بعد التمرين: [المنتج] ← «روتين بسيط… بيفرق»
>
> **EN:** 6:30 → alarm → [product] coffee in 3 minutes → gym bag: [product] + water → empty streets → last set → after training: [product] → "Simple routine. Makes a difference."

**Shot list**

| # | Time | Framing | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2 s | Phone screen close-up | Alarm 6:30 being switched off |
| 2 | 2–6 s | Top-down, kitchen | Making the coffee or opening the snack (product clearly visible) |
| 3 | 6–9 s | POV | Packing the gym bag, product goes in |
| 4 | 9–12 s | POV | Walking out; empty street at dawn |
| 5 | 12–17 s | Neck-down | Last set at the gym |
| 6 | 17–22 s | Close-up | Eating or drinking the product after training |
| 7 | 22–25 s | Product shot | End card |

**Camera:** handheld at chest height for POV; lock exposure on the street shot so the sky doesn't blow out. **Light:** kitchen window or an under-cabinet light; dawn light outside is the look. **Edit:** cut on the music beat; text bottom-centre inside the safe zone; warm grade.

**Caption:** AR «صباح القاهرة ☀️ #روتين_الصبح» · EN "Cairo mornings ☀️" · **CTA:** «اطلبه من اللينك» / "Get yours from the link."

## 6. Fitness apparel: "Squat, sweat and stretch test"

- **Who films:** you, neck-down (men's tee or shorts). For women's leggings, a female roster creator, filmed respectfully: mid-distance framing, no close-ups of the body beyond what the test needs.
- **Shows brands:** product-proof format; fits Gym Apparel, Sigma Fit, Haxel, Mavin Wear, Magma.
- **Length:** 25–30 s.

**Hooks**
1. AR: «أصعب ٣ اختبارات لأي لبس جيم» · EN: "The 3 hardest tests for any gym wear."
2. AR: «تيشيرت قطن قصاد بوليستر بعد ٢٠ دقيقة» · EN: "Cotton vs polyester after 20 minutes."
3. AR: «بيستحمل السكوات؟» · EN: "Does it survive the squat?"

**Script (Egyptian Arabic)**
> [0–3] أصعب ٣ اختبارات لأي لبس جيم. خلينا نشوف.
> [3–9] اختبار ١: السكوات قدام لمبة قوية. [النتيجة الحقيقية: مابيشفّش / بيشفّ شوية].
> [9–16] اختبار ٢: العرق بعد ٢٠ دقيقة كارديو. [النتيجة الحقيقية].
> [16–21] اختبار ٣: الشد… ويرجع لشكله؟ [النتيجة الحقيقية].
> [21–27] ٣ من ٣؟ [الحكم الصادق]. اسم البراند في أول كومنت.

**Script (English)**
> [0–3] The 3 hardest tests for any gym wear. Let's see.
> [3–9] Test 1: squat under a bright light. [real result].
> [9–16] Test 2: sweat after 20 minutes of cardio. [real result].
> [16–21] Test 3: stretch. Does it bounce back? [real result].
> [21–27] 3 out of 3? [honest verdict]. Brand name in the first comment.

**Shot list**

| # | Time | Framing | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3 s | Neck-down, full body | Standing in the outfit, title text |
| 2 | 3–9 s | Side angle, bright light | Slow squat, held at the bottom |
| 3 | 9–16 s | Mid shot | Before and after 20 minutes of cardio (two clips, same angle) |
| 4 | 16–21 s | Close-up of hands | Fabric stretched and released |
| 5 | 21–27 s | Neck-down, three angles | Fit check: front, side, back; verdict ticks |

**Camera:** tripod at hip height for full-body shots, 2–3 m away; same position for before/after so the comparison is fair. **Light:** one strong light or a bright window from the side for the squat test. **Edit:** "TEST 1/2/3" titles; split-screen before/after; ticks or crosses appear on each result.

**Caption:** AR «٣ اختبارات لأي لبس جيم 🏋️ النتيجة؟» · EN "3 tests every gym outfit should pass 🏋️" · **CTA:** «اسم البراند في أول كومنت» / "Brand name in the first comment."
