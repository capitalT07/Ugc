## Usage rights in plain words

- **Organic:** the brand can post the videos on its own accounts and website for as long as it wants.
- **Paid ads:** the brand can run the videos as ads for the period paid for, counted from delivery. After that, they stop running them as ads or buy an extension.
- **Never included for free:** unlimited or perpetual paid use, TV, outdoor, packaging, reselling the videos, using a creator's face or voice in other campaigns, editing a creator into new claims, and any AI cloning of a creator's face or voice.
- **Ownership:** the brand gets a licence to use the final videos. Raw footage and project files stay with the studio unless bought.

## Pricing strategy

1. **Lead with the Creative Test.** Three concepts is what a brand needs to find a winning ad. Starter is the low-risk door; Sprint is the anchor you mention first so the Creative Test feels reasonable.
2. **First-order bonus, not a discount.** Two extra hooks free on a first order paid by **Sunday Nov 22**. Discounts make you look cheap and are hard to undo.
3. **Cut scope, not price.** If they push back, offer fewer videos or no paid usage at the same per-video rate.
4. **Always quote in writing** with deliverables, delivery date, revisions and usage period.
5. **Product costs:** the brand ships the product and pays shipping. The creator keeps it unless the brand asks for it back at its own cost.

Creators who film **and** edit are paid per finished video: EGP 1,000–2,000 for newer creators. I use **EGP 1,200** as the planning figure, close to the Egyptian nano-creator range of EGP 500–1,500 per video ([03](03-market-evidence.md#1-prices)). Extra hook lines cost EGP 150 each.

On USD packages the creator share is only about 20–30%, which is why dollar clients matter so much.

Creators are paid per finished video **out of the client's payment**, within 48 hours of client approval. You never pay a creator before the client has paid you. Details in [09-creator-roster.md](09-creator-roster.md).

## Revenue math

Assumptions (mine, adjust as real numbers come in): FX EGP 54 per USD; creator pay EGP 1,200 per finished video (≈ $22) plus EGP 150 per extra hook line; about 3% fees on USD payments (Payoneer, Upwork, wire), close to 0 on InstaPay.

| Scenario | Mix | Sales | Creator pay | Fees | **You keep** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≈ $1,000 in sales | 1 Sprint + 1 Creative Test + 1 Starter (USD) | $970 | $214 | $29 | **≈ $727** |
| $1,000 kept | 2 Sprints + 1 Creative Test (USD) | $1,370 | $317 | $41 | **≈ $1,012** |
| Egypt only | 2 Sprints + 2 Creative Tests + 2 Starters (EGP 53,000) | ≈ $981 | ≈ $428 | ≈ 0 | **≈ $554 (EGP 29,900)** |
| $2,000 kept | 4 Sprints + 2 Creative Tests (USD) | $2,740 | $633 | $82 | **≈ $2,025** |

What this means:

- **Dollar clients come first.** Agencies abroad, Gulf brands with no-shipping formats and Upwork jobs keep about 70% of the price for you; Egyptian packages keep about 55%.
- **Egypt-only pricing can't reach $1,000 kept this week.** At about 56% kept, it would take roughly EGP 96,000 in sales.
- **Timing:** by Nov 22, realistic cash is deposits and 100%-upfront packages. Sprint balances and Mostaql earnings (14-working-day hold) mostly land the week after. The dashboard tracks each stage separately.

## Activity math (assumption, not research)

Six days of about 165 personalised touches plus 30 applications. If 8–12% reply (15–20 replies), a third of those turn into a call or quote (5–7 quotes), and a third to a half close, that is **2–3 paying clients**. Check these rates on Day 3 against real replies and adjust volume or messaging.
