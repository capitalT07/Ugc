## 2. Demand

- **Mostaql:** 22 Arabic UGC and Reels/TikTok editing projects; the newest (IDs 128xxxx) look recent. Several ask for Saudi or Gulf dialect.
- **Upwork:** 12 relevant posts. Freshest: AI UGC for an e-commerce brand (Nov 12), plant app UGC (Oct 20), street-interview ads (Oct 15). Many others are months old by my estimate from their job IDs.
- **Companies hiring for UGC and video:** Convertedin ("UGC Specialist" for Egypt, KSA, UAE; claims 800+ e-commerce clients) ([job](https://www.founditgulf.com/job/ugc-specialist-converted-egypt-42303127)); The Hair Addict (content creator, [Wuzzuf](https://wuzzuf.net/jobs/p/kusoinhlgyvx-social-media-specialist-and-content-creator-the-hair-addict-cairo-egypt)); Whitecollars (Arabic DTC ad editor); Scale Army (remote editors in Egypt).
- **Brands growing through TikTok ads:** Cotmasr in Egypt (14x ROAS, self-reported) ([case study](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/cotmasr-tiktok-success-stories)); Fzahabaya and Swag Store in Saudi Arabia.
- **Regional market:** the Middle East influencer market is mature; Egypt is Facebook-first with TikTok growing fast; TikTok Shop is live in Saudi Arabia and the UAE ([Kolsquare, Apr 2026](https://www.kolsquare.com/en/blog/influencer-marketing-in-the-middle-east-in-2026-high-stakes-high-spend-and-the-arabic-first-imperative)).
- **Egyptian local brands are rising** against imports, many selling through Instagram and TikTok storefronts ([Egyptian Streets, Jul 2026](https://egyptianstreets.com/2026/07/08/local-brands-storm-egypts-retail-scene/); Enterprise's "Made in Egypt" series, Oct–Nov 2026).
- **Creator platforms in the region:** UGC MENA (450+ creators, free to join, $100 minimum USD payout in Egypt), Stllr (KSA, UAE, Egypt), Arabic Creators, MENA Creators, Collabstr (≈ 36–49 UGC creators listed in Egypt).

## 5. AI UGC (checked Nov 16)

- **Performance:** Ipsos (May 2026, 3,000 US consumers) found human-made ads 14% stronger short term; a Taboola field study (Jan 2026) found AI ads level on click-through; human UGC tends to win for physical-product demos and testimonials ([AdTestBench](https://adtestbench.com/guides/ai-ugc-vs-human-ugc/), [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/ai-generated-ads-performance/)).
- **Labels:** TikTok and Meta require disclosure of realistic AI people in ads in 2026 and reject undisclosed ones (vendor summaries: [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/ai-disclosure-policy-for-tiktok/), [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/ai-labeling/)).
- **Fake testimonials:** the US FTC rule (in force since Oct 21, 2024) bans testimonials from people who don't exist, with fines up to $53,088 per violation ([Crowell](https://crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/final-rule-announced-the-ftc-strengthens-its-enforcement-capacity-against-deceptive-reviews-and-testimonials)). I did not research Egyptian or Gulf advertising law.

## 6. Findings vs assumptions

**Findings (sourced above):** price ranges, platform fees and holds, the open projects and hiring signals, brands' ad activity, AI disclosure rules.

**My assumptions (adjust with real data):**
- Reply rate 8–12%, a third of replies to a quote, a third to a half of quotes closing.
- Creator pay EGP 800–2,500 per video.
- About 7–8 hours of your time per Creative Test.
- FX of EGP 54 per USD and about 3% fees on USD payments.
- Posting dates estimated from Mostaql and Upwork ID sequences.
- "Converted" in the job post = Convertedin.

## 7. What I could not check

- **Social platforms:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit and Upwork pages were blocked by my environment's network policy (Reddit is also closed to the search crawler). So: no profile audits, no Meta Ads Library checks, no feed observations, and Upwork details came from search snippets.
- **Whether each project is still open.** Check before applying.
- **Brand handles and websites** not named in a source are left blank in the CRM.
- **Links:** checked for format only; live checks were blocked.
