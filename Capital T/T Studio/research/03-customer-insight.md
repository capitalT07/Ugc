## Analysis (Thu Nov 26)

- **User vs. buyer:** who uses the videos and who pays.
- **Jobs to be done:** functional, emotional and social, in the form "When … I want to … so I can … and feel …".
- **Pains, ranked:** with how many people named each one.
- **Moments that start the search:** for example a product launch, ads that stop working, or a creator who let them down.
- **Price anchors:** what they paid and how they judged it.
- **What earns trust**, and the **objections** to expect.
- **Segments:** do agencies, brands and apps answer differently?
- **Each insight is tested:** is it true, is there a tension, can we act on it?
