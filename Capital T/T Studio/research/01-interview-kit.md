# Interview kit: what Egyptian brands need from video ads

**Goal:** 8–10 answers by Wed Nov 25 from people who buy or make video ads. We learn how they make videos today, what's hard, and what they pay, before T Studio sells anything.

## Rules

1. **These are questions, not a pitch.** No prices, no packages, no "we can do this for you". If someone asks what T Studio sells, answer briefly and honestly, then go back to their answers.
2. **The owner approves each message, then sends it themselves.** At most 12 people, each message personal (first name and one line about their brand), never copy-pasted in bulk.
3. **Ask about what they did, not what they would do.** "What did you pay last time?" beats "Would you pay X?" People are polite about the future and accurate about the past.
4. **Write down their exact words.** Paste replies or screenshots to Claude. The research files keep the words but no names, handles or numbers. Names stay in your DMs or your private sheet.
5. **Give something back.** Promise a short summary of what you learned from all the brands, and send it at the end of the week.

## 1. Opening message

**To a brand (Instagram DM from @tstudioo.ugc), Egyptian Arabic:**

> أهلاً [first name] 👋 أنا [NAME] من T Studio. بنبدأ ستوديو صغير لإعلانات الفيديو في القاهرة، وقبل ما نبيع أي حاجة عايزين نفهم إزاي البراندات المصرية بتعمل فيديوهات الإعلانات بتاعتها دلوقتي.
> ممكن أسألك ٥ أسئلة سريعة؟ تقدر ترد كتابة أو فويس، وهياخدوا حوالي ٥ دقايق. وهبعتلك ملخص اللي هنعرفه من كل البراندات لو حابب.

English: "Hi [first name], I'm [NAME] from T Studio. We're starting a small video-ad studio in Cairo, and before we sell anything we want to understand how Egyptian brands make their video ads today. Could I ask you 5 quick questions? Reply in text or a voice note; about 5 minutes. I'll send you a summary of what we learn from all the brands, if you'd like."

**To an agency (email or LinkedIn), English:**

Before we sell anything, we're asking agencies how they get short Arabic video ads made for their clients today. Could I ask you 5 quick questions by message? It takes about 5 minutes, and I'll share a summary of what we learn.

**To someone you know who sells online (WhatsApp), Egyptian Arabic:**

> أهلاً [first name] 👋 بعمل بحث صغير عن إزاي البراندات بتعمل فيديوهات الإعلانات، ومحتاج رأيك بصراحة. ممكن ٥ أسئلة سريعة؟

## 2. The five questions

Send them all in one message after they say yes, or one by one if they prefer.

| # | Egyptian Arabic | English | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | إزاي بتعملوا فيديوهات المحتوى والإعلانات دلوقتي؟ مين بيصورها ومين بيعمل المونتاج؟ | How do you make your video content and ads today? Who films and who edits? | Their current solution: in-house, freelancer, agency or influencer |
| 2 | بتحتاجوا كام فيديو جديد في الشهر تقريباً؟ وبتنزل كإعلانات ممولة ولا بوستات عادية؟ | About how many new videos do you need a month? Do they run as paid ads or normal posts? | Volume, and whether the need is recurring and tied to ad spend |
| 3 | إيه أصعب حاجة في الموضوع ده؟ تلاقي الناس، الأفكار والسكريبت، الجودة، التكلفة، السرعة، ولا إن الفيديو يبيع؟ | What's the hardest part: finding people, ideas and scripts, quality, cost, speed, or getting videos that sell? | The real pain, and whether scripts and ideas matter (our differentiation guess) |
| 4 | آخر مرة دفعتوا في فيديو واحد كام تقريباً؟ ونفع ولا لأ؟ | Roughly what did you pay for one video last time, and did it work? | A real price anchor and their sense of results |
| 5 | لو حد هيمسك الموضوع ده عنكم، إيه اللي لازم يوريهولكم أو يضمنه قبل ما تجربوه؟ | If someone took this off your hands, what would they have to show or promise before you'd try them? | What earns trust: samples, price, speed, a refund or a first test |

**If they're happy to talk more** (call or voice note), the best follow-ups are:
- "Tell me about the last time you needed a video."
- "What did you do about it?"
- "Why?"

## 3. Follow-up and thank-you

**After 2 days with no reply (once only):**

> أهلاً تاني [first name] 👋 عارف إنكم مشغولين. لو عندك دقيقتين للأسئلة اللي فاتت هكون ممنون جداً، ولو الوقت مش مناسب مفيش أي مشكلة.

English: "Hi again [first name], I know you're busy. If you have two minutes for my questions I'd be very grateful, and if not, no problem at all."

**After they answer:**

> شكراً جداً على وقتك 🙏 لما نخلص البحث آخر الأسبوع هبعتلك ملخص اللي عرفناه.

English: "Thank you so much for your time. When we finish the research at the end of the week, I'll send you a summary of what we learned."

## 6. What would change our mind (agreed before the answers come in)

Writing this down first stops us from reading the answers the way we hope. Proposed thresholds, for you to adjust:

- **Keep or narrow the studio idea** if most people (5 or more of 8):
  - need videos regularly, 4 or more a month or every ad campaign;
  - **and** name finding people, scripts and ideas, quality or time as a real pain;
  - **and** have paid about EGP 1,500 or more for a single video.
- **Narrow to a niche** if one group (for example agencies, or activewear brands) answers far more strongly than the rest.
- **Change the offer** if most make videos themselves for free, don't run paid ads, or say price is the only thing that matters.
