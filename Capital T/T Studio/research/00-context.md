## 1. Product overview

- **Name:** T Studio (تي ستوديو), always shown as "T Studio · Arabic UGC ads". It's the first business of Capital T.
- **What it sells:** short vertical video ads (15–45 s) that look like real people using a product.
  - T Studio writes the script and hooks.
  - A creator films and edits.
  - T Studio checks every video before delivery.
  - The videos are Arabic-first (Egyptian dialect), with English and French where they fit.
- **Model:** a done-for-you service. Creators are paid per finished video from the client's payment.
- **Packages** ([02](../docs/02-offer-and-pricing.md#packages)):

  | Package | What's included | EGP | USD |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Starter | 1 video | 3,500 | 120 |
  | Creative Test | 3 videos | 9,000 | 330 |
  | Content Sprint | 5 videos + 5 extra hooks | 14,000 | 520 |

- **Stage:** launched Nov 17, 2026, with no clients, portfolio, reviews or revenue yet. Brand outreach is paused for this research week.
- **Channels:**
  - Instagram `@tstudioo.ugc` and TikTok `@tstudio_ugc`;
  - Gmail `Tstudio.eg@gmail.com`;
  - a landing page, published as a private link so far;
  - WhatsApp Business is waiting.
- **Cost side:** creators on Khamsat start at $5–90 a video. An edited video within $35 is rare: 1 of 9 listings checked ([03 §9](../docs/03-market-evidence.md#9-finding-creators-without-contacts-checked-nov-17)). The planning figure is EGP 1,000–2,000 per finished video ([02](../docs/02-offer-and-pricing.md#what-creators-cost-you-internal-never-show-clients)).

## 4. Pain points and problems (hypotheses)

- **Creative fatigue:** ads stop working after a short time, and new videos are needed constantly.
- **Finding reliable creators:** they don't know whom to trust; quality, dialect and deadlines vary.
- **Not knowing what to say:** writing a hook and script that sells is a skill most founders don't have.
- **Time:** briefing, shipping the product, chasing and revising take hours.
- **Cost and risk:** paying for videos that don't sell.

What Khamsat buyers praise suggests what they worry about. In 176 public buyer reviews on 21 UGC listings, read Nov 21:
- 40% mention professionalism and cooperation;
- 23% mention image, sound or editing quality;
- 22% mention speed or delivery on time;
- 15% say they'll buy again.

Only 1 review has a reservation ("needs to be longer"). Public reviews are mostly praise, so **real pains need interviews.** `[REAL DATA NEEDED]`

## 5. Competitors

Full table and sources: [02-competitors.md](02-competitors.md).

- **Direct (managed UGC for brands):**
  - Stllr: Riyadh platform; claims 5,000–7,000+ creators.
  - UGC MENA: brands pay a 15% or 25% fee.
  - Arabic Creators: reported 20% commission.
  - Collabstr: Cairo creators at $50–300.
  - influUGC: Giza agency serving Egypt and Saudi Arabia, **the closest local rival**.
  - Dubai agencies such as Hypebox (projects from about $1,000).
  - Fiverr "Arabic UGC agency" sellers ($50–200 a video).
- **Indirect (same problem, different solution):**
  - hiring a creator directly on Khamsat ($5–90 starting price) or Mostaql (projects budgeted at $50–250);
  - influencers;
  - the brand's own staff filming on a phone;
  - an in-house hire;
  - AI video, which is rising on Mostaql but crowded.
- **Biggest threat:** the brand hiring a creator directly, cheaper. "We do UGC" is not a difference by itself.

## 6. Differentiation (three hypotheses, no proof yet)

1. **Scripts and hooks written to sell, in Egyptian Arabic,** not just a person talking to camera.
2. **One accountable studio:** the right creator picked, briefed and checked, so the brand deals with one person, with no chasing and no surprises.
3. **Built for testing:** several hooks per ad, so a brand learns what sells.

Only 3% of the Khamsat reviews mention ideas or scripts, so buyers may not yet value point 1. The interviews must test it. `[REAL DATA NEEDED: proof for each point]`

## 7. Barriers and anti-persona

**Likely objections (hypotheses):**
1. "Why not hire the creator myself on Khamsat for $25–50?"
2. "You have no portfolio or reviews yet."
3. "Will it actually sell?"
4. "Shipping the product to a creator is a hassle."
5. "It's too expensive."

**Not a customer:**
- a brand that doesn't run ads and wants one cheap video;
- anyone wanting a famous influencer, fake testimonials or AI people passed off as real customers;
- editing-only work.

## 10. Brand voice

**Tone:** honest, simple and practical; Egyptian Arabic for Egyptian brands, plain English elsewhere ([04](../docs/04-portfolio-scripts.md), [05](../docs/05-sales-system.md)).

**Say:**
- "we write the script and hooks, a creator films and edits, we check every video";
- prices in the client's currency;
- "concept ad · not sponsored" on test pieces.

**Never say:**
- that the owner films or edits;
- any promise of sales, views or return on ad spend;
- anything that presents an AI person as a real customer.
