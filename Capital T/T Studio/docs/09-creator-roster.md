# Creator roster kit

**Creators film and edit; you don't.** You recruit, cast, brief, check and pay. I write the scripts and hooks.

> **Privacy:** keep creators' real names, phone numbers and IDs in your private Google Sheet, **not** in this repository. `crm/creators.csv` here is only the empty template.

## Who to recruit (3–5 people this week)

Look for people who **already film and edit their own short videos** (CapCut is fine). Their own TikToks or Reels are the proof.

| Slot | Why |
|---|---|
| 1 man, 20s–30s, gym-goer | Activewear, grooming, supplements, apps |
| 1 woman, 20s–30s | Beauty, fragrance, hair, modest fashion (opens many leads) |
| 1 person with curly hair | Bobana, The Hair Addict |
| 1 natural talker, any age | Reviews, storytelling, street-style reactions |
| Optional: neutral or Gulf Arabic speaker, or French speaker | Gulf and francophone briefs |

**Adults only (18+).** Check an ID before the first paid job.

**Where to find them (you don't need to know anyone):**
1. **Khamsat's [UGC videos category](https://khamsat.com/video-design/ugc-videos):** creators, many of them Egyptian, who already film and edit UGC and sell it with samples, prices and reviews. Start here: [Hiring on Khamsat](#hiring-on-khamsat).
2. **Instagram and TikTok:** searches such as `#ugccreator`, `#ugcegypt`, `#صانعة_محتوى`, `#صانع_محتوى`; message them from T Studio's account.
3. **Collabstr's Egypt and Cairo UGC lists** (link in Hunting Links), mainly for dollar clients.
4. People you know who post their own videos, if there are any.

Contact strangers only through public business contacts or platform messages.

## Hiring on Khamsat

Searches on Nov 17 found Khamsat's UGC sellers mostly starting around $25 a video ([03](03-market-evidence.md#9-finding-creators-without-contacts-checked-nov-17)).

**Shortlist 8–10 who:**
- speak Egyptian Arabic in their samples;
- appear on camera themselves (skip listings made with AI);
- deliver edited videos: hook text, captions, clean cuts, music;
- charge up to $35 a video (up to $50 only for dollar clients). "Starting at" prices are for a basic video, so check the extras for length, extra hooks and raw clips;
- have some reviews and reply quickly.

Keep their usernames, links, prices and delivery times in the Creators tab of your private Google Sheet, not in this repo.

**Message the best 5 through Khamsat (AR)**
> أهلاً 👋 أنا [NAME] من T Studio، ستوديو إعلانات UGC في القاهرة. شفت نماذج شغلك هنا وعجبتني.
> بنجهّز فريق صغير لإعلانات براندات مصرية: إحنا بنكتب السكريبت والهوكات، وصانع المحتوى بيصوّر ويعمل المونتاج.
> قبل ما أطلب، محتاج أعرف:
> ١. الفيديو بيكون باللهجة المصرية؟
> ٢. سعر فيديو ٣٠–٤٥ ثانية متصوّر ومتعمله مونتاج (كابشن ومزيكا) كام؟ وكام لو عايز نسختين زيادة بهوك مختلف؟
> ٣. التسليم بياخد كام يوم بعد ما المنتج يوصل؟ وينفع الكليبات الخام تتبعت كمان؟
> ٤. في مانع إن الفيديو يتعرض كإعلان ممول على صفحات البراند، مع ورقة موافقة استخدام قصيرة؟
> الطلب والدفع هيكونوا من خلال خمسات. شكراً!

**Khamsat's rules, and ours**
1. Messages, orders and payment stay on Khamsat. Offering to pay outside the site, or pushing a seller to move to WhatsApp, breaks its rules and loses its protection.
2. Order only after the client has paid you. You pay by card or PayPal; a buyer fee shows at checkout.
3. Their samples are the audition: never ask for a free video. The first paid order is the test.
4. Send the [production brief](#production-brief-send-for-every-job) and the [release](#creator-release-and-work-agreement) in the order chat, and ask them to agree to the release there before filming.
5. Khamsat guarantees an order for 14 days after delivery: check the video against the QA checklist in [07](07-delivery-workflow.md) before you accept it.
6. Buy only: don't list services on Khamsat. Its terms stop sellers from taking orders and fulfilling them through another seller, and the penalty is account suspension ([03](03-market-evidence.md#9-finding-creators-without-contacts-checked-nov-17)).
7. Under Khamsat's terms the buyer owns the full rights to delivered work unless the listing or the agreement says otherwise before work starts. Ask the creator in the order chat to confirm nothing is reserved, then pass the video to your client under your agreement.

## Audition

**Creators from Khamsat:** skip this; their samples and answers are the audition. For creators found elsewhere, ask for two things:
1. **Two videos they already filmed and edited** (any topic), plus whether you may show them in T Studio's portfolio, labelled as their earlier work.
2. **A 20–30 second self-tape** of the script below, **edited the way they'd edit an ad**: hook text in the first second and captions.

State up front: the audition is **unpaid, never posted, never used in any ad**. It may be shown privately to a brand to choose a creator, and is deleted after casting.

**Script (Egyptian Arabic)**
> «بصراحة كنت فاكر إن [المنتج] ده زي أي حاجة تانية… بس بعد أسبوع، أكتر حاجة عجبتني إن [الميزة]. لو إنت زيي و[المشكلة]، جربه.»

**Script (English)**
> "Honestly, I thought this was just another [product]... but after a week, what surprised me was [benefit]. If you're like me and [problem], try it."

## Scorecard (1–5 each; recruit at 28/40 or above)

You don't need editing skills to score this; [10-studio-basics.md](10-studio-basics.md) explains what to look for.

| Criterion | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Natural on camera | reads the script | sounds like their own words |
| Picture and sound | dark, echo | bright face, clear voice |
| Editing | slow start, no captions | hook text in the first second, clean captions, no dead pauses |
| Follows the brief | ignores instructions | every instruction followed |
| Speed | 3+ days to reply | same-day |
| Reliability | vague | gives and keeps a time |
| Dialect and language fit | unclear | clear Egyptian, neutral Arabic or English |
| Niche fit | no link to your leads | uses or fits the product types |

## What you pay creators (keep internal)

| Item | EGP |
|---|---|
| Finished video (filmed + edited), newer creator | 1,000–2,000 (plan with 1,200) |
| Finished video, experienced creator | up to 3,000 |
| Finished video bought on Khamsat | $25–35 plus the buyer fee ≈ 1,460–2,000; $40–50 (≈ 2,270–2,840) only for dollar clients |
| Extra hook line, filmed and cut into its own version | 150 each |
| Raw clips | included |
| Reshoot or re-edit because of a creator mistake | free |
| Reshoot because the client changed the brief | 50% of the video rate |
| Paid-usage extensions you sell to the client | pass 30–50% of the extension to the creator |
| Product | creator keeps it unless the brand wants it back (brand pays return shipping) |

## Payment rules

1. Creators are paid **only from money the client has already paid you.** Never front cash.
2. Creators you hire directly: pay within **48 hours of client approval**, by InstaPay or bank transfer, with a receipt screenshot. Khamsat creators: pay through Khamsat when you place the order, after the client has paid you.
3. Record every amount in the Creators sheet (Owed / Paid / Balance).
4. If the client cancels after filming, the creator still gets 50% from the client's deposit (on Khamsat, its cancellation rules apply).

Khamsat amounts assume EGP 54 per $ and a buyer fee of 5%, minimum $2, which a competitor reports; Khamsat shows the real fee at checkout.

## Creator release and work agreement

This is a plain-language template, not legal advice. Each creator signs once (WhatsApp photo of a signed copy or an e-signature is fine), and you send a short job confirmation for each job (product, fee, usage period).

**English**

> **Creator Release and Work Agreement**
>
> Between T Studio (the Studio), represented by [NAME], and ____________ (the Creator), ID no. ________, age 18 or over.
>
> 1. **Work.** For each job the Studio sends a written brief. The Creator films and edits the agreed videos on time and sends a watermarked preview, then the final edited videos (each hook version) and the raw clips.
> 2. **Fee.** The fee per job is stated in the job confirmation. The Studio pays within 48 hours of the client's approval. Reshoots or re-edits caused by the Creator are free; reshoots caused by client changes are paid at 50%.
> 3. **Licence.** The Creator lets the Studio and its client use the Creator's image, voice and the videos for the platforms and period in the job confirmation. Organic posting on the client's own accounts has no time limit; paid ads run only for the paid period. Any longer or wider use needs the Creator's written approval and extra payment.
> 4. **Limits.** The Creator's face or voice will not be used to train AI, cloned, or edited into statements the Creator did not make. No political, religious, adult or sensitive-health use. No claims the Creator hasn't agreed to.
> 5. **Honest use.** The Creator only says what is true about their own use of the product.
> 6. **Own accounts.** If the Creator posts a video on their own account, they label it as paid partnership or ad.
> 7. **Portfolio.** Both sides may show concept-ad swaps and, after the client's campaign is live and with the client's permission, client videos.
> 8. **Confidentiality.** Briefs, prices and unreleased products stay private.
> 9. **Exclusivity.** None, unless a job confirmation states it and pays for it.
> 10. **Taxes.** Each party handles its own taxes.
> 11. **Removal.** After the paid period ends, the Studio asks the client to stop running the ads.
> 12. **Law.** Egyptian law applies.
>
> Signed: __________ (Studio) __________ (Creator) Date: ________

**العربية**

> **اتفاق عمل وتصريح استخدام — صانع/ة محتوى**
>
> بين T Studio «تي ستوديو» (الاستوديو)، ويمثله [NAME]، و ____________ (صانع/ة المحتوى)، رقم قومي ________، والسن ١٨ سنة أو أكتر.
>
> ١. **الشغل:** لكل مهمة بيبعت الاستوديو بريف مكتوب. صانع المحتوى بيصوّر ويعمل مونتاج للفيديوهات المتفق عليها في الميعاد، ويبعت نسخة معاينة عليها علامة مائية، وبعدها الفيديوهات النهائية (كل نسخة هوك) واللقطات الخام.
> ٢. **الأجر:** أجر كل مهمة مكتوب في تأكيد المهمة، والاستوديو بيدفع خلال ٤٨ ساعة من موافقة العميل. إعادة التصوير أو المونتاج بسبب صانع المحتوى مجانية، وإعادة التصوير بسبب تغيير العميل بتتدفع بـ٥٠٪.
> ٣. **التصريح:** صانع المحتوى بيسمح للاستوديو وعميله باستخدام صورته وصوته والفيديوهات على المنصات وللمدة المكتوبة في تأكيد المهمة. النشر العضوي على حسابات العميل من غير مدة، والإعلانات المدفوعة للمدة المدفوعة بس. أي استخدام أطول أو أوسع محتاج موافقة مكتوبة وأجر إضافي.
> ٤. **حدود:** ممنوع استخدام وش أو صوت صانع المحتوى لتدريب ذكاء اصطناعي أو نسخه، أو تعديله لكلام ماقالوش. ممنوع الاستخدام السياسي أو الديني أو للبالغين أو للادعاءات الصحية الحساسة.
> ٥. **الصدق:** صانع المحتوى بيقول بس الحقيقة عن تجربته مع المنتج.
> ٦. **حساباته الشخصية:** لو نشر الفيديو على حسابه، بيكتب إنه إعلان أو شراكة مدفوعة.
> ٧. **البورتفوليو:** الطرفين ليهم يعرضوا الإعلانات التجريبية المتبادلة، وفيديوهات العملاء بعد نزول الحملة وبموافقة العميل.
> ٨. **السرية:** البريفات والأسعار والمنتجات اللي لسه ما نزلتش سرية.
> ٩. **الحصرية:** مفيش، إلا لو اتكتبت في تأكيد المهمة واتدفع مقابلها.
> ١٠. **الضرايب:** كل طرف مسؤول عن ضرايبه.
> ١١. **الإيقاف:** بعد انتهاء مدة الإعلانات، الاستوديو بيطلب من العميل يوقفها.
> ١٢. **القانون:** القانون المصري هو المطبق.
>
> التوقيع: __________ (الاستوديو) __________ (صانع المحتوى) التاريخ: ________

**Job confirmation (per job, WhatsApp)**
> Job {id} · {brand} · {n} videos, filmed and edited · fee EGP {fee} · paid ads {days} days from delivery · preview by {date} · final files after the client pays.

## Production brief (send for every job)

1. **Product and buyer:** what it is, who buys it, the one feeling the ad should leave.
2. **Script:** the full script, plus 3 hook lines to record separately.
3. **Shot list:** numbered shots (see the templates in [04-portfolio-scripts.md](04-portfolio-scripts.md)).
4. **Editing spec:** the list in [04](04-portfolio-scripts.md#editing-spec-send-to-creators-check-every-video-against-it): hook text in the first second, captions in the safe zone, music cleared for ads, end card.
5. **Do / don't:** claims allowed, words to avoid, no other brands' logos, no music while filming.
6. **Technical:** vertical 9:16; 4K or 1080p at 30 fps; phone back camera; lens wiped; face a window or ring light; quiet room; 3 takes of each line.
7. **Deliverables, in this order:** (a) a watermarked 720p preview (`PREVIEW · T Studio`); (b) after the client approves and pays, the final edited video in each hook version plus the raw clips.
8. **Delivery:** through Google Drive, or WhatsApp *as document* (not as video, which compresses it), by {date and time}.

## Safety and ethics

- Adults only; check ID; no minors in any shot.
- The creator must actually try the product. No invented results.
- No medical, financial or "guaranteed result" claims.
- Never ask for anyone's passwords; never share client logins with creators.
- First in-person meetings in public places.
