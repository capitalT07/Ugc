# Payments, client agreement and protection

## What to use

| Client | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Egyptian brand or agency | **InstaPay or local bank transfer (EGP)** | Instant, no platform hold, almost no fees |
| Gulf or international client you trust | **Payoneer payment request** or **USD wire** to your Egyptian bank | Direct, days not weeks |
| Stranger abroad you can't verify | **Upwork contract** | Escrow protects both sides; you pay the fee for that safety |
| Mostaql or Upwork job | Stay on the platform | Off-platform deals break their terms and remove protection |

**Cash by Nov 22 can only come from direct payments.** Mostaql holds earnings 14 working days after delivery; Fiverr clears in 14 days; UGC MENA pays after brand approval with a $100 minimum.

## Payment rules for every direct deal

1. **Starter and Creative Test: 100% upfront. Sprint: 50% deposit, 50% before final files. Monthly retainers: paid at the start of the month.**
2. Nothing is filmed until the deposit lands in *your* account (not a screenshot, the money).
3. Previews are **watermarked and lower resolution**. Clean final files go out only after full payment.
4. Creators are paid only from money already received ([09-creator-roster.md](09-creator-roster.md)).
5. Record each stage in the Revenue Ledger: signed, deposit, delivered, balance, landed in bank.

## Client service agreement (direct clients)

Plain-language template, not legal advice. Send as a PDF or Google Doc; a WhatsApp "Approved" reply plus the deposit is the minimum acceptance.

**English**

> **UGC Production Agreement**
>
> **Studio:** T Studio, represented by [NAME] ("we"). **Client:** ____________ ("you").
>
> 1. **Deliverables.** Package: ________. ___ videos, ___–___ seconds, 9:16 (4:5 on request), with ___ hooks, captions and licensed music.
> 2. **Timeline.** We send scripts within 2 working days of receiving the product and brief. You approve or comment within 24 hours. We deliver ___ days after script approval. Delays in product delivery or approvals move the date by the same amount.
> 3. **Payment.** Total ________ (EGP/USD). Schedule: ________ (100% upfront / 50% deposit and 50% before final files). Method: ________. Work starts when the deposit arrives.
> 4. **Revisions.** One round per video: trims, text, captions, music, choice of takes. A new concept, script or re-shoot after approval is new work, quoted separately.
> 5. **Usage.** You may post the final videos on your own accounts and website with no time limit. You may run them as paid ads on ________ for ___ days from delivery. Longer or wider use (TV, outdoor, packaging, other brands, resale) needs a written extension at the listed price.
> 6. **Ownership.** You get a licence to use the final videos as above. Raw footage and project files stay with us unless you buy them (+30%).
> 7. **Creators.** The people in the videos are adults who agreed to this use. You will not use their face or voice for AI training or cloning, edit them into statements they did not make, or use the videos for political, religious, adult or sensitive-health content.
> 8. **Your responsibilities.** You provide accurate product information and only ask for claims you can support. You are responsible for your ad accounts, ad approvals and any required "ad" or "AI" labels.
> 9. **Previews and delivery.** Previews are watermarked. Final files are delivered after full payment.
> 10. **Cancellation.** Before filming: refund of payments minus 15% for scripting. After filming: the deposit is not refundable, and finished videos are delivered on payment of the balance. If we cancel, you get a full refund.
> 11. **Late delivery.** If we deliver more than 5 working days late for reasons on our side, you may cancel the undelivered videos for a refund of their share.
> 12. **Portfolio.** We may show the videos in our portfolio after your campaign is live, unless you say no in writing.
> 13. **Confidentiality.** Unreleased products, prices and results stay private.
> 14. **Liability.** Our total liability is limited to the fees you paid under this agreement.
> 15. **Law.** Egyptian law applies.
>
> Accepted: __________ (Client) __________ (Studio) Date: ________

> ٩. **المعاينة والتسليم:** النسخ للمعاينة عليها علامة مائية، والملفات النهائية بتتسلم بعد الدفع الكامل.
> ١٠. **الإلغاء:** قبل التصوير: استرداد المدفوع ناقص ١٥٪ مقابل السكريبت. بعد التصوير: المقدم مش بيرجع، والفيديوهات الجاهزة بتتسلم بعد دفع الباقي. لو الاستوديو لغى: استرداد كامل.
> ١١. **التأخير:** لو الاستوديو اتأخر أكتر من ٥ أيام عمل لسبب من عنده، للعميل يلغي الفيديوهات اللي ماتسلمتش ويسترد نصيبها.
> ١٢. **البورتفوليو:** للاستوديو عرض الفيديوهات في أعماله بعد نزول الحملة، إلا لو العميل رفض كتابةً.
> ١٣. **السرية:** المنتجات اللي لسه ما نزلتش والأسعار والنتائج سرية.
> ١٤. **المسؤولية:** أقصى مسؤولية على الاستوديو هي قيمة المدفوع في الاتفاق ده.
> ١٥. **القانون:** القانون المصري هو المطبق.

## Scam checklist (refuse every one)

- **Overpayment or cheque:** "we sent too much, refund the difference". Never refund; it's fake money.
- **Buy equipment or software from "our vendor"** and get reimbursed later.
- **Moving money for someone:** receiving and forwarding payments makes you a money mule.
- **Account rental:** anyone asking to use your Upwork, Mostaql, TikTok, PayPal or bank account.
- **Pay to unlock work:** registration, training, "verification" or "starter kit" fees.
- **Fake brand deals:** emails with `.zip`, `.rar`, `.exe`, `.scr` "briefs", or links asking you to log in to a "brand portal". Open briefs only as Google Docs or Drive previews; never run downloaded files; check the sender's domain matches the brand's website.
- **"Like and earn" task jobs** on Telegram or WhatsApp.
- **Crypto-only payment** from strangers.
- **"Send the final files first, we pay after"** from someone new. Send watermarked previews only.
- **Requests for passwords or one-time codes.** Never share them.
- **Trading, forex or crypto promotions:** only licensed products, no profit promises, or skip.
- **Off-platform payment before a contract** on Upwork or Mostaql.
