# Studio basics in 30 minutes

You won't film or edit. You need three things: talk about the work with clients, brief creators clearly, and spot a weak video before a client does.

## 1. What a UGC ad is

A short vertical video (15–45 seconds) that looks like a real person sharing a product, made to run as an ad on TikTok, Instagram or Snapchat. It works because it feels like a recommendation, not a commercial. Brands buy many of them because each ad wears out after a few weeks of spending.

## 2. Hooks

**What a hook is:** the first 1–2 seconds of the video: the first line, the first image and the text on screen. Most viewers decide in that moment whether to keep watching.

**"3 hooks per ad":** the same video with 3 different openings. The brand runs all three and keeps the winner. Creators film the extra opening lines in the same session, so it costs little to make.

**Eight formulas that work**

| Formula | Arabic example | English example |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | «لو بشرتك بتلمع بعد الضهر…» | "If your skin gets shiny by noon…" |
| Result first | «ده الكيرلي بتاعي يوم ٣ من غير ما ألمسه» | "My curls on day 3 without touching them." |
| Test | «اختبار السكوات قدام لمبة قوية» | "Squat test under a bright light." |
| Comparison | «قطن مصري قصاد بوليستر بعد ساعة تمرين» | "Egyptian cotton vs polyester after an hour of training." |
| Mistakes | «٣ غلطات بيعملها أغلب الرجالة مع زيت الدقن» | "3 mistakes most guys make with beard oil." |
| Honest doubt | «كنت فاكره زي أي برفان تاني…» | "I thought it was just another perfume…" |
| Price or value | «طقم جيم كامل بأقل من ١٠٠٠ جنيه» | "A full gym outfit under EGP 1,000." |
| Curiosity / POV | «POV: أول أسبوع بالمنتج ده» | "POV: my first week with this." |

**Rules for every hook**
- True for the creator who says it. No invented results.
- One idea, said in under 2 seconds.
- Product visible within the first 3 seconds.
- Specific beats clever: numbers, places, real situations.
- No medical, financial or "guaranteed" claims.

**Picking between hooks** (I'll usually send you 3): read each one aloud. Keep the ones that sound like something a friend would say; drop anything that sounds like a TV ad. You're the judge of natural Egyptian Arabic, not me.

## 3. What good filming looks like (to check, not to do)

| Check | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Face lit evenly from a window | Dark face, bright window behind, yellow and white light mixed |
| Sound | Clear voice, no echo, no fan or AC noise | Echo, wind, music playing while filming |
| Framing | Vertical 9:16, face or product fills the frame | Horizontal, tiny subject, head cut off |
| Stability | Tripod or steady hands | Shaky when it shouldn't be |
| Product | Clearly visible, label readable, really used | Hidden, unopened, pretend use |
| Background | Tidy, no other brands' logos | Mess, competitor products |

## 4. What good editing looks like (to check, not to do)

1. Hook text on screen from the first frame (4–7 words).
2. Something changes every 1–2 seconds (new shot, zoom or text); no dead pauses.
3. Captions on every spoken line, spelled right, away from the bottom and the right edge where TikTok and Reels put their buttons.
4. Voice louder than the music; music from a library cleared for ads, not a trending song.
5. Product in the first 3 seconds and again at the end with the call to action.
6. Vertical 1080×1920; no CapCut ending clip; watermark only on previews, never on finals.

The full list is the [QA checklist](07-delivery-workflow.md#qa-checklist-before-any-preview-goes-out).

**Giving feedback to a creator:** name the time and the fix, all in one message. For example: "0:00–0:02 hook text missing · 0:14 caption says X, should be Y · music too loud under the voice."

## 5. How to explain the process to a client

- **AR:** «إحنا بنكتب السكريبت والهوكات، وبنختار صانع محتوى شبه عملائكم يصوّر ويعمل المونتاج، وبنراجع كل فيديو قبل ما يوصلكم. وكل إعلان معاه ٣ بدايات مختلفة عشان تختبروا أنهي بتبيع أكتر.»
- **EN:** "We write the script and the hooks, cast a creator who looks like your customers, and they film and edit it. We check every video before you see it, and each ad has 3 different openings so you can test which one sells."
- **If asked "who edits?":** "Our creators edit their own videos to our spec, and we check every one before delivery."

Never say you film or edit yourself.

## 6. Train your eye (10 minutes a day)

Open TikTok Creative Center → Top Ads (link in the Hunting Links sheet) and filter by Egypt or Saudi Arabia and your categories. For 5 ads a day, note the first line, what's on screen in the first second, and how fast it cuts. After a week you'll spot a weak video at a glance.

## 7. Later: learn CapCut basics (optional)

Not needed this week. If you later want to make small fixes yourself (swap a hook, fix a caption), three practice sessions are enough:
1. Import a clip, cut the pauses, add text in the first second.
2. Auto captions in Arabic and English; fix the errors; move them into the safe zone.
3. Duplicate the project and change only the first 2 seconds to make a second hook version; export 1080×1920.

Practise on your own footage, never on a client job.
